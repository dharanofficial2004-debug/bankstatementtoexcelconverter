## Cannibalization and duplicates

- Previously, ten French routes largely competed on general conversion/extraction. The CSV route had a genuine format intent but repeated the same template. No byte-identical route files were found; duplication was structural and semantic.
- General PDF-to-Excel intent now belongs to `/fr/convertir-releve-bancaire-en-excel`. Home is a branded French guide hub. Other routes cover choice, CSV, OCR, field extraction, Excel import, multipage handling, validation, cash-flow organization and troubleshooting. Accent and grammatical variants are grouped within these purposes.
- All 88 FAQ questions and all article paragraphs are distinct. Shared navigation, process explanations and the explicitly fictional example are deliberate common product context.
- The older `/fr/convertisseur-releve-bancaire-excel` had the most existing content. Without Search Console or backlink data, the selected main URL is an editorial recommendation rather than a proven authority winner. Historical slugs remain; review their performance after deployment.
- No merge, redirect, deletion or cross-page canonical consolidation was performed.

## Internal links and technical fixes

- Removed ten fabricated English hreflang targets formed from French slugs. All pages now declare only verified French self references; no speculative English/Spanish/Portuguese equivalents.
- Replaced four-link synonym lists with relevant guides and a hub linking every existing French page. Corrected the old “PDF vers CSV” destination, which pointed to the generic converter. Every French page has incoming links.
- Local HTTP and fragment checks found no broken links. This does not establish production availability before deployment.
- Unique absolute titles avoid the inherited brand suffix pushing titles out of range. French OG/Twitter title, description, locale and a 1200×630 PNG are supplied for each route.
- Self canonicals and sitemap share one no-trailing-slash URL policy. Removed generated-at-build lastModified timestamps. Local trailing slash URLs normalize in one 308 hop.
- FAQ schema exactly matches expandable visible answers; detail routes have matching visible breadcrumbs and BreadcrumbList. WebPage declares French. Organization/WebSite are inherited once from the root.
- French-only shell replaces English marketing navigation/footer/video and HDFC/UPI example; no fake upload control is added. The existing converter opens from the first screen.
- Removed unsupported speed, accuracy, universal compatibility, deletion, no-login and unlimited-use claims.

## Remaining issues and limits

- Shared root `<html lang="en">` remains; all French page content has a server-rendered `<div lang="fr">`. A full document-language solution needs coordinated root-layout architecture across locales; nested html elements or client-only language fixes were not added.
- Root SoftwareApplication structured data still injects global USD offer amounts. It was not changed because pricing and shared-site semantics require separate validation. French pages add no offer, review, rating or integration claims.
- No verified reciprocal EN/ES/PT translation clusters exist for the newly specialized French guides. Spanish/Portuguese bank hubs are not exact counterparts; guessed alternates were removed instead of linking unrelated pages.
- The /app, preview, auth and pricing experiences remain in their current language. French CTAs explain that the converter and linked pricing page are in English. Export headers remain unchanged and are explained accurately.
- Live apex/www pages could not be fetched with the web tool during review. Hosting-level redirects, Google-selected canonicals, actual indexation, impressions, CTR and search volumes remain unverified. Local validation is not evidence of deployment.
- Product capabilities were checked against source, not by uploading bank data or performing a payment. External OCR availability and production retention policies were not independently validated.
- No legal/privacy route was found to link; content accurately explains known remote processing and browser persistence without inventing a retention guarantee.
- No ranking or FAQ rich-result guarantee. Google limits FAQ rich results mainly to authoritative government and health sites.
